# Research log

This log is reconstructed only from the copied project record.

- **Question:** can coherent photonic temporal features support the locked NARMA-10 benchmark while retaining an auditable physical-evidence boundary?
- **Alternatives investigated:** ideal coherent delay, sparse temporal feature selection, realistic receiver/noise modelling, robust optimization, and several splitter/combiner geometries.
- **Key decision:** keep P5 blind performance frozen and evaluate P6 physical evidence without tuning to blind scores.
- **Rejected / unresolved:** generic coupler, Y-splitter, MMI, and initial inverse-design paths did not clear the required component gates; foundry-qualified S-parameters and several device inputs remain required.
